[details]
I built an Arduino-based ultrasonic sonar and paired it with an RFID access-control setup. Bringing both modules into one project let me explore distance sensing and card-based access together.
The HC-SR04 estimates distance from the time taken by an ultrasonic pulse to return. The RFID reader checks a tag before the access-control output responds. Working through both parts helped me practise sensor interfacing and basic embedded-system integration.
Reliable operation depends on a clear path for the sonar echo and a readable RFID tag. The project gave me a useful starting point for thinking about how sensing and authentication can share a controller.
