# Smart Agriculture Model  (page: project-smart-agriculture-model.html)
order: 2
title: Smart Agriculture Model
category: Automation
summary: A smart farm prototype with automatic irrigation, a food dispenser and a secure RFID entrance.

[details]
I designed and put together this smart farm prototype to show how automation can make farming easier.
It has three main parts: an automatic irrigation setup, a food dispenser, and an RFID card system that keeps the farm entrance secure.

[features]
Automatic irrigation setup
Food dispenser
RFID card system for the farm entrance

[tools]
Automation, RFID, Microcontroller
