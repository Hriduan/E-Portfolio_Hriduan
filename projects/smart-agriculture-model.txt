[details]
I put together this small farm model to explore a few everyday uses of automation. It combines an automatic irrigation setup with a food dispenser in one prototype.
I also added an RFID card system at the entrance to control access. Building the model let me bring these separate functions together in a practical setup.


[features]
Automatic irrigation setup
Food dispenser
RFID card system for the farm entrance

[tools]
Automation, RFID, Microcontroller
