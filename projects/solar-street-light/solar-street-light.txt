[details]
I built a solar-powered street light using basic components such as diodes and op-amps. I wanted to put my electronics knowledge to use in a simple renewable-energy project.
The setup also includes a phone charging point. Combining lighting and charging in one prototype gave me a practical way to explore solar energy.
