[details]
I built a small solar-powered street light using basic components such as diodes and op-amps. The project gave me a practical way to explore renewable-energy electronics.
I also added a phone charging point to the setup. Bringing lighting and charging together helped me see how one power source can serve two everyday needs.

Solar-powered lighting
Street-light control using diodes and op-amps
Phone charging point
