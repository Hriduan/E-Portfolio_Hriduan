image_note: Technical diagrams of the signal path, reader interface, control circuit and verification flow. They document the intended architecture rather than claim to be photographs of the prototype.

[details]
I wanted to explore how electronics could make voter checks more consistent. For this project I built a voting machine that scans a voter's NID card and verifies the person automatically.
The idea was to check the voter before allowing a vote rather than depend entirely on manual checks. My goal was a more secure and reliable voting process.


[features]
Scans NID cards
Automatic voter verification
Made for a secure and reliable voting process

[tools]
Electronics, Microcontroller, NID card scanning
