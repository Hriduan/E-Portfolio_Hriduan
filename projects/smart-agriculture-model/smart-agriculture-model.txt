# Smart Agriculture Model  (page: project-smart-agriculture-model.html)
order: 2
title: Smart Agriculture Model
footer_title: Smart Farm Automation Model
category: Automation
summary: I combined proximity sensing and RFID access with automatic feeding and soil-moisture-based irrigation.
image_note: These photos and figures come from the project report and show the prototype wiring and reported test results.

[details]
I developed the model with Md. Abdullah Abrar, Samiul Alim Siam and Aftabul Kanzan. An Arduino Uno brings four sensing tasks together: an HC-SR04 checks for nearby people, an MFRC522 reader checks RFID cards, an HW-201 infrared sensor detects an animal at the feeder and a resistive probe tracks soil moisture.
The controller wakes a 16x2 LCD when someone comes within 30 cm. A small servo opens the food gate when the infrared sensor detects an animal. For irrigation the soil probe controls a mini pump through a 5V relay. The report describes a two-threshold control band: the pump turns on above an ADC reading of 620 and switches off below 580.
During workbench testing the report records 52 mA in standby and 114 mA with the display awake. The setup also has practical limits: direct sunlight can interfere with the infrared sensor, the exposed soil probe can corrode and simultaneous motor loads can pull down the supply voltage.

[features]
Ultrasonic proximity sensing wakes the display
RFID reader controls access
Infrared sensor and servo automate the food gate
Soil moisture probe and relay control irrigation

[tools]
Arduino Uno, HC-SR04, MFRC522 RFID, HW-201 IR, HL-69 soil probe, SG90 servo, 5V relay, 16x2 I2C LCD
