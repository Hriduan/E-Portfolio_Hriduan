[details]
I worked with Md. Sabbir Uzzaman Khan on a MATLAB study of audio noise reduction. We added white Gaussian noise at 15 dB SNR then compared low-pass and band-pass FIR filters in MATLAB R2024a.
The low-pass filter was designed around a 4 kHz passband and a 4.4 kHz stopband. It reduced high-frequency hiss but also made the audio quieter and more muffled. The band-pass filter kept the speech range from 300 Hz to 4 kHz while rejecting frequencies below 200 Hz and above 4.5 kHz. Speech stayed clearer and louder although noise remained where it overlapped with the voice.
The time and frequency plots made the main trade-off easy to hear: simple filters can reduce noise but they cannot remove noise that shares the same frequencies as speech. Fixed cutoffs also limit how well the design can handle different recordings.
