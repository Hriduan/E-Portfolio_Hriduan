# Solar Street Light & Phone Charger  (page: project-solar-street-light.html)
order: 3
title: Solar Street Light & Phone Charger
category: Renewable energy
summary: A solar-powered street light that also works as a mobile phone charging station.

[details]
For this project I used basic components like diodes and op-amps to create a functional street light powered by solar energy.
The same setup also works as a convenient charging station for mobile phones.

[features]
Powered by solar energy
Street light built with diodes and op-amps
Mobile phone charging point

[tools]
Solar energy, Diodes, Op-amps
