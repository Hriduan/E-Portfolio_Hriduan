About me

Here is a little about my studies and the work I enjoy.

I am a final-year Electrical and Electronic Engineering student at AIUB with a CGPA of 3.87. My interest in VLSI grew as I started working with digital designs. I have completed the mandatory VLSI course and the elective ASIC Design course at my university.

For my FSMD controller project I wrote the design in SystemVerilog and used Cadence tools for synthesis and timing-path simulation. Working through the design helped me see how RTL connects to an ASIC or FPGA implementation. I enjoy tracing unexpected behaviour back to its cause and want to build my career in the VLSI industry.

I also led my group at VLSITHON 3.0. Alongside my coursework I build practical electronics projects and take part in engineering events. I learn best when I can try an idea myself and talk through what worked with the people around me.
